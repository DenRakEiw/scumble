## The three musts

- **`canvas-tools/filter-layer-stale-empty-px-after-geometry`** (wrong pixels in exports and paid runs; export/run refusal above 268 MP). Plain filter layers (`l.kind === "filter"` with `!ownPx(l)`) keep an empty `l.px` allocated at the canvas dimensions from when the layer was added, and none of the five whole-document geometry operations (`turnDocumentNow`, `straightenDocumentNow`, `cropCanvasNow`, `resizeImageNow`, `extendCanvasNow`) updates `l.px` to `nw × nh`. All three mask-creation paths (`maskFromSelection`, `fillLayerMask`, `maskFromLayer`) size `l.maskPx` from `layer.px.width × layer.px.height` rather than `this.width × this.height`. After any aspect-changing 90° turn or crop before masking a filter layer, `drawTilesInto` uses separate `fx` and `fy` scales so the screen looks right, whereas `stackPlan` rejects `fm.width !== this.width` and `flattenToCanvas` applies a single scalar `ms = layer.maskPx.width / this.width` to both axes—squashing the mask in flat exports and paid-run crops (and throwing above 268.4 MP); upscaling permanently downsamples new filter masks to the pre-upscale resolution; and `extendCanvasNow` skips shifting stale-sized masks while marking `l.dirty = true` so `syncLayersOnce` uploads the stale empty `l.px`. Small fix: replace `l.px = this.pixels.Layer.empty(nw, nh)` on plain filter layers in all five geometry operations, size new filter masks from `this.width × this.height`, and do not mark plain filter layers `dirty` in `extendCanvasNow`.
- **`relight-depth-key/depth-map-raw-floats-in-state-json`** (data loss: `.scumble` save refused and session autosave halted). All three depth paths (`computeDepthMap`, `depthFromPoints`, and `depthFromLayer` at `WORK_MAX = 4096`) keep a live `Float32Array` on `map.meta.raw`, and `mapToJSON` runs `JSON.parse(JSON.stringify(map.meta))`, expanding millions of floats into a `{"0":...,"1":...}` object on every `getValue()`. After *Depth from layer* (even on a 1920 × 1280 image, and up to 11.18M–16.78M floats on 4K–15k) or *Finest (3×3)* depth on 4K+, `scumble/document.json` exceeds `docfile.js`'s 64 MiB `MAX_HEADER` cap so `Ctrl+S` refuses to save the document (with no UI action to remove `maps.depth`), while on a 4096 × 4096 square image or multi-tab session `JSON.stringify(this.bundle())` exceeds V8's max string / 512 MiB IPC limit and throws inside `saveAll()`, silently stopping `autosave.json` for every open tab—even though a reloaded plain-object `meta.raw` is rejected by `instanceof Float32Array` anyway. Small fix: keep `raw`, `rw`, `rh` off serialized `map.meta` (strip typed arrays / `raw` in `mapToJSON` or hold them in a non-serialized slot).
- **`host-gen/split-landing-full-canvas-loses-paid-run-and-bloats-15k`** (money lost above 268 MP; multi-GB main-thread spike at 15k). The 0.1.57 `splitSendSize` fix scales large pictures down before `splitSizeRefusal` and dispatches the billed Seedream split call, but `readyRow` still draws every returned layer onto an unchecked `row.w × row.h` `<canvas>` (`canvasOf`) and runs `canvasBytes(c)` on the window. Above Chromium's 268.4 MP canvas limit (`editor.huge`), `c.toBlob` resolves `null` on `rows[0]` after the paid call has finished, throwing `TypeError` on `null.arrayBuffer()`, catching `"Nothing was added."`, calling `dropLayerParts`, and losing all paid layers; at 15000 × 10000 (150 MP) on WaveSpeed / Oxen / OpenRouter (up to 16 full-frame layers + base) it allocates up to 17 × 600 MB = 10.2 GB of unzeroed GPU canvases on the window, while `sourceBytes` on a layer split still calls `editor.layerPixels(job.layer)` (`toCanvas()`). Small fix: land returned split layers at model resolution with layer box `(row.x, row.y, row.w, row.h)` (or stream into `TileLayerPixels` via the worker pool), zero scratch canvases on error/completion, and route layer-source `sourceBytes` through `sampleRegionSettled`.

- **`p1-deeppaint-layer/plain-limit-darkens-over-transparency`** (bug, small) `renderer/editor/inpaint_limit.js:41` `limitStage`: When a plain Deep paint layer (opacity 100%, normal blend, no layer mask or clip) has a feathered depth, luma, or colour limit (`params.limit`) over transparent or semi-transparent pixels, `limitStage` interpolates straight RGBA channels linearly (`mix(i, c, w)`). Because `applySurface` composites paint `over` transparent input (`c.a > i.a`) without setting `def.over`, `mix((0,0,0,0), (t, 1), w)` multiplies straight RGB by `w` as well as setting alpha to `w`, turning feathered limit edges dark/black on screen, in exports, and in banded run composites.
