Observations:
- `png_read unfilter` drops from `1,101.6 [1,083.1-1,161.4] ms` to `502.1 [499.1-573.2] ms` in `open_the_file` (2.19x,
  saving ~600 ms) and from `1,098.8 [1,078.7-1,185.6] ms` to `474.5 [469.8-596.2] ms` in `open_again` (2.32x, saving
  ~624 ms), with non-overlapping 3-run ranges.
- Because `readPng` drains `DecompressionStream` while unfiltering each 256-row band, cutting `unfilter` by ~600 ms
  reduces `png_read write` (feed back-pressure) by the same ~630-660 ms (`2,419.7 -> 1,789.4 ms` and `2,327.9 ->
  1,666.4 ms`) and `png_read kernel` by ~630-660 ms (`2,489.4 [2,433.6-2,648.8] -> 1,859.0 [1,786.6-2,210.5] ms`, 1.34x,
  and `2,395.5 [2,319.7-2,649.3] -> 1,734.0 [1,679.7-2,152.1] ms`, 1.38x, both with non-overlapping 3-run ranges).
