2. What a key evicts, and the next Generate's reload (raw/b0_evict.json)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

(ck_model 10.0 s) is not in the rows. smi = nvidia-smi MiB before -> after.

- **Size-independent.** Klein's three models (12,863 MiB by nvidia-smi: 17,913 against 5,050 idle) leave the GPU at
  every key. ComfyUI's log shows "Requested to load Flux3TEModel_ / Flux3 / AutoencoderKL" and the loader nodes run
  again in every Generate after a key, as after /free. In a warm Generate the loaders are cached and nothing loads. The cost is +2.9-3.0 s on Klein, whatever the
  key's size; a larger model pays its own cold load (not measured).
- **Why** (read in ComfyUI 0.44.0, matching the rows): a prompt drops from the node cache the outputs of nodes it does
  not contain (execution.py:764 `clean_unused`). The other workflow's model patchers die, and the model manager frees
  them (`cleanup_models_gc` in free_memory, model_management.py:893-1070). The key's own request did not unload
  anything at 1 MP. Pack A's make_room asks free_memory for its estimate (6.3 GB), free_memory unloads only while free
  VRAM is below that (model_management.py:909-921), and 17.5 GB were free. The same holds the other way: a Generate
  drops CorridorKey's loader, so the next key reads its 4.65 GB of weights again (8.9-10.0 s from the OS file cache).
  Back-to-back keys keep it cached (section 3: ck_model cached, 2.26-2.43 s at 1 MP).
- **What it means for Route B** (for B2, B3 and B5's texts): a key right after a Generate costs about 11.5 s at 1 MP,
  13 s at 4 MP and 17 s at 16 MP, not I0a's warm 2.65 / 2.89 / 6.60 s. The next Generate pays about 3 s on Klein.
  keep_on_gpu does not avoid the loader's rerun after another prompt (ck_model 9.66 s with it on). It only saves the
  weights' trip between keys in a row (I0a: 0.72 s against 2.65 s), so it suits repeated Re-keys. After a Generate it
  leaves 1.75 GiB in torch's pool beside the Generate's models (torch_vram_total; nvidia-smi 19,589 against
  17,913 MiB), until /free with free_memory (5,053 MiB after it).
- **KEY_CAP = 16 MP.** The eviction gives no reason to go lower. On an empty card the 16 MP key peaks at 14,257 MiB by
  nvidia-smi (about 9,200 MiB over the 5,038 MiB idle; section 3's uncancelled reference). The key node takes 6.0 s warm, and
  17.1 s wall after another prompt. The pack keys it in its normal mode: its own estimate, 23.2 GB x 1.1 + 0.3 = 25.8 GB,
  fits the 28.9 GB budget it computes on this card. By the same formula (runtime.py's _VRAM_PEAK_GB, read) normal mode
  lasts up to about 19.3 MP here (inferred from the formula, not run). On a 24 GB card the pack's rule would put 16 MP
  into its low_vram mode (inferred; no such card here). The cap is about this card and the plan's 8,192 px side limit;
  a cap by the card's VRAM is B1's call.
- Not run: 2 and 8 MP, hint / no hint per size (the plan's condition did not occur; b0.py keeps the `extra` phase for
  them).

3. A Cancel, and whether the interrupt flag stays set (raw/b0_cancel.json)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Scumble's own path (host.js cancelComfyPrompt: POST /api/jobs/<id>/cancel, never a bare /interrupt). Each cancelled
16 MP key used a fresh input, then a 1 MP key of another still ran at once. The loader was cached through this phase
(no other workflow in between).

| case | cancel sent (s after post) | the answer | the prompt ended | the next key |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 16 MP uncancelled (reference) | - | - | success at 7.24 s (ck_key 6.04) | - |
| 0.5 s into the key node | 0.88 | 200 {"cancelled": true} | interrupted in ck_key at 1.99 s | success, 2.35 s (ck_key 2.26) |
| 3.0 s into the key node | 3.35 | 200 {"cancelled": true} | interrupted in ck_key at 4.39 s | success, 2.37 s (ck_key 2.28) |
| 5.4 s into the key node | 5.73 | 200 {"cancelled": true} | ck_key finished (6.08 s), interrupted at ck_out (PreviewImage) at 6.42 s, no output | success, 2.55 s (ck_key 2.43) |
| after its end (1 MP) | 2.35 (= the end) | 200 {"cancelled": false} | success, the output kept | success, 2.35 s |
| a 1 MP key pending behind a 16 MP one | at once | 200 {"cancelled": true} | the pending job never ran, no history entry; the 16 MP key beside it success, 7.17 s | - |

- **The flag does not leak.** ComfyUI 0.44.0 resets it at the start of every prompt (execution.py:739), and the job
  endpoint interrupts only the running job with that id, under the queue's mutex (execution.py:1380-1397). The rows
  agree: the key after each cancel ran as fast as an uncancelled one. History status: "error" with messages
  execution_start, execution_cached, execution_interrupted. The websocket sends execution_interrupted with node_id and
  node_type.
- **Latency:** pack A checks for the interrupt only at its step starts (pipeline.py: the RVM pass, each stage-1 window,
  each stage-2 batch; read). So a cancel lands 1.0-1.1 s after the click early and mid-run. In the last part (stage 2
  started) the key runs to its end, and the executor's check before PreviewImage stops the prompt 0.68 s after the click.
  Either way nothing is written for Scumble to fetch: comfyPictureRun's "interrupted" path is the one taken.
- **VRAM after a cancel:** 5,0xx MiB (idle) every time. The pack's `active()` offloads in a finally block (loader.py),
  so an interrupted key leaves nothing on the GPU.
- **Progress frames** (for B3's bar): 3 `progress` frames per key from the pack's ProgressBar (value 1..3, max 3).
  At 16 MP 1/3 arrives about 1.2 s into the prompt and 3/3 at 3.4 s of 7.2 s, so the bar stands full for the last
  ~3.8 s (the decode, the despill, the PNG). At 1 MP 3/3 comes at 1.2 s of 2.35 s. A cancel 0.5 s in saw no frame.
