2. The reference keys (dist/ck_ref/<still>/<case>/, untracked; every file's sha256 in manifest.txt)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- How: scripts/ck_ref.py on python_embeded (-I -B), ComfyUI stopped. It loads pack A's vendored upstream `corridorkey`
  package by path (not ck_comfy) and pack A's ck_comfy/env.py by file for the two loaders upstream's foundations.py
  needs on transformers 5 (MoGe under a private name with the utils3d shim, C-RADIO built eagerly with its pinned
  hf_model.py); then upstream runtime.load_model (17.8 s, 4.65 GB of weights on the GPU) and upstream's path for a
  one-frame clip in normal mode, step by step: probe_frame_dir_meta / preprocess_frame_dir_path (8-bit PNG / 255,
  display sRGB and the asinh code, both bf16, reflect-101 to a multiple of 128), RVM as precompute_rvm_to_memmap
  (bilinear to H/4, zero state, normalised, stored fp16), C-RADIO + MoGe inline as hann_stage1_stream
  (_build_foundation_inputs, float32 out), load_initial_hint_frame for the hint, stage 1 under bf16 autocast (the baton
  stored fp16 as upstream's memmap), stage 2 under inference_mode + bf16 autocast, the decode of
  _render_predictions_torch in float.
- Cases per still: b768 and b1024 (the bucket input made from the box: INTER_AREA, OpenCV 4.13.0 of python_embeded)
  and native (the still itself, 1344 x 1344, padded by upstream to 1408 x 1408, 32 px each side), each without a hint
  (zeros, quality -1) and with the rough brush hint at quality 0.75 (the pack's "rough matte" preset). 5 x 6 = 30 keys.
- The hint (hint_full.png per still, cropped and reduced per bucket the same way, 8-bit grey, white = subject):
  a quick screen-difference key, gaps under 8 % of the side closed, holes filled, grown by 3 %, painted over with round
  dabs of radius 4 % on a grid, blurred by side / 400 (scripts/ck_common.py). It overshoots the subject by about 90 px
  and misses the plant's top-right leaf (lit close to the screen's green): kept as a rough hint's flaw; the key has the
  leaf anyway.
- The box (§4.7 points 1-2): with a hint, the hint's bounds x 1.5, at least 768 and at least the bucket's side, square,
  placed in the picture, clamped; without one, the whole picture. On these square stills every box is the whole picture
  (the subjects fill the frame), so no reflect padding was needed at any bucket and the hint / no-hint inputs of a
  bucket are the same bytes (the hint differs).
- Files per case: in/input.png (the exact 8-bit RGB input the run consumed; native: the still), hint.png (with a hint),
  feat_cradio.npy (1152 ch), feat_moge.npy (768 ch), feat_rvm.npy (384 ch) at /64, baton.npy (512 ch at /16),
  aux.npy (stage 1's 4 ch at /2), logits.npy (the raw 4-channel stage-2 output, bf16 in torch), all float16 CHW at the
  padded model size; alpha.npy (HW) and fg_linear.npy (HWC RGB, scene-linear, straight, unclamped) float16 cropped to
  the input; alpha.png and fg.png 8-bit (round(x * 255); fg sRGB-encoded from linear clamped at 0, no despill);
  case.json (box, scale, pads, shapes, dtypes, min / max / NaN / Inf per array, seconds per part, peak memory).
  C-RADIO and MoGe features are fp16 roundings of the float32 stage 1 received; RVM and the baton are exactly what
  upstream feeds (its fp16 memmaps).
- NaN / Inf: none in any array of the 30 keys, the highlights still included; no array overflows float16. Largest
  |value| over all keys: feat_cradio 27.5, feat_moge 5.7, feat_rvm 5.8, baton 4.3, logits -0.41 .. 1.28, fg_linear
  164.9 (the asinh decode of a logit near 1.28, linear light far above 1; fp16 holds it).
- Noise floor: hair_green and highlights_green (12 keys) run a second time in a fresh process: every array and every

- The mode: normal (low_vram=False) at 1, 4 and 16 MP on this card with ComfyUI otherwise idle; one window, one
  forward segment. The pack's estimate (23.2 GB at 16 MP) is far above what the samples saw (about 9 GB over idle).
- keep_on_gpu off: the weights travel to the GPU and back every run (about 1.8 s of the 2.4 s warm key at 1 MP).
  keep_on_gpu on: 4.6 GB stay (nvidia-smi 4,430 -> 9,007 MiB; ComfyUI torch_vram_total 0.23 -> 5.07 GB).
  /free {"unload_models": true} alone does NOT release them (8,874 MiB, torch_vram_total unchanged);
  /free {"unload_models": true, "free_memory": true} does (4,236 MiB, torch_vram_total 0.23 GB): the pack's model is
  no ModelPatcher, only the node cache reset drops it.
- The rgba read-back (Basic, 1 MP, the subject hint, clean-matte trust, despill 0.5 green; PreviewImage -> /view):
  an 8-bit RGBA PNG (bit depth 8, colour type 6), 1024 x 1024; its RGB equals the `foreground` output byte for byte
  and its A equals the `alpha` output (through MaskToImage) byte for byte; straight, not premultiplied (3.5 % of the
  pixels have RGB above alpha + 2); where alpha is 0 (183,457 px) the RGB is near black (mean 1.3 of 255), so nothing
  may be read from the colour under a zero alpha. (foreground and alpha come back as 8-bit RGB PNGs, colour type 2.)
- The polarity of a grey hint through ImageToMask (channel red) into alpha_hint, clean-matte trust: white = subject.
  The subject hint gives alpha 0.996 inside its white area and 0.013 outside; the inverted hint gives 0.003 inside and
  0.988 outside (the model follows the hint, both ways).

5. Where this differs from the row, or what the row did not say
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The stills are not the user's: none were in the repo, so they were made with FLUX 3 Image Klein on the user's
  ComfyUI (the step's instruction). The "user's plate" of §4.2's by-eye hair check is hair_green.
- The stills are square, so the box plan's growth to a bucket's aspect and its edge padding were not exercised (every
  box is the whole picture); the native case exercises upstream's own 128 padding. A rule was added to §4.7 point 1's
  box for the reference: at least the bucket's side, so the bucket resample is always a reduction (INTER_AREA).
- hint_quality 0.75 for the brush hint (the row names none).
- fg_linear is saved besides the 8-bit fg (the row says "decoded fg"; linear is what the model gives, the PNG is
  sRGB). aux.npy (stage 1's /2 output) is saved as well, for a preview from stage 1 alone (§2.1).
- The Route B 16 MP input is a 3x Lanczos upscale of a 1.8 MP still (the cost is that of 16 MP; the content is soft).
- The Route B polarity and read-back hint was made by the first version of the rough hint (dilate + dabs, without the
  close and the hole fill); it is in dist/ck_ref/routeb/hint_1024.png.
- ComfyUI got --disable-auto-launch beside the bat's flags.
- The recipe flux3_klein_local's default text encoder (qwen3vl_4b_int8_convrot.safetensors) is not on the user's
  server; qwen3vl_4b_bf16.safetensors was set for the stills. Not changed in the recipe (a note for whoever owns it).
