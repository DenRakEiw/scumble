K33 — Stitch colour match & alpha blend in Rust (2026-11-08)
============================================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §6 K33)
--------------------------------------
- Two stitch kernels in `crates/px/src/maskf.rs` (scalar + wasm32 `simd128` in module `lanes`), exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs` (ABI bumped 25 -> 26):
  1. `color_match_rgba(patch_ptr, reference_ptr, weight_ptr, n) -> u32`:
     - Clamps each `weight[i]` to `[0, 1]` when summing `total` in `f64` (preserving `NaN` so `NaN < 64.0` is `false` and propagates to `0` RGB stores). If `total < 64.0`, returns `0` and leaves `patch` untouched.
     - Otherwise accumulates weighted means (`r_mean[3]`, `t_mean[3]`) and variances (`r_v[3]`, `t_v[3]`) in `f64` in sequential pixel order (`0..n`), skipping `wt <= 0.0` while using the unclamped positive `wt` (`(dd * dd) * wt`), matching `stitch.js` `colorMatch` bit-for-bit.
     - In `simd128`, `lanes::color_match_stats` pairs `[reference[j + c], patch[j + c]]` into the two lanes of `f64x2` (`m0, m1, m2` and `v0, v1, v2`), computing both images' statistics in one pass over `weight` while keeping every accumulator's addition order 100% sequential.
     - Computes `std = sqrt(max(v / total, 1e-6 * 255 * 255))`, clamps `scale[c] = std_r / std_t` to `[0.5, 2.0]`, and transforms RGB in place with ties-to-even rounding (`f64x2_nearest` in SIMD, `round_ties_even()` in scalar) clamped to `0..255` (`Uint8ClampedArray` semantics), preserving `patch` alpha bytes (`patch[4*i + 3]`). Returns `1`.
  2. `blend_alpha(src_ptr, mask_ptr, dst_ptr, n, keep_alpha)`:
     - Copies RGB from `src` to `dst` (when `src != dst`) and replaces each pixel's alpha byte:
       - `keep_alpha == 0` (`stitch.js` `alignCopy` & `finalAlpha` without `keepAlpha`): `Math.round(clamp01(mask[i]) * 255)`.
       - `keep_alpha != 0` (`stitch.js` `finalAlpha` with `keepAlpha`): `Math.round(clamp01(mask[i]) * src[4*i + 3])`.
- JS twins `colorMatchRgba` and `blendAlpha` in `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js`, `Px` wrappers in `renderer/editor/px/px.js`, and routed wrappers in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` under routes `"colorMatch"` and `"blendAlpha"` (`ROUTE_KINDS.colorMatch = "kernel"`, `ROUTE_KINDS.blendAlpha = "kernel"`).
- Wired into `renderer/editor/stitch.js` (`colorMatch`, `alignCopy`, and `finalAlpha` in `finishPixels`, with `colorMatch` also exported for direct testing).

4. Decision Rule (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §1.1)
-----------------------------------------------
Both `color_match_rgba` and `blend_alpha` win in the kernel microbenchmarks (`2.07x` and `1.35x` at `1492 x 1492`) and across the stitch end-to-end benchmarks (`stitch_align_2048` worker wall `-102.59 ms` / `-13.4%`, `stitch_align_4096` worker wall `-376.62 ms` / `-14.1%`, warmed `native_test provider_crop` wall `-38.00 ms` / `-7.1%` and `finish.SUM` `-49.59 ms` / `-11.9%`). Both `"colorMatch"` and `"blendAlpha"` stay on `"rust"` in `DEFAULT_ROUTES`.
