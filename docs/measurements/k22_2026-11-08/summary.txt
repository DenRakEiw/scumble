K22 measurement summary (2026-11-08)
====================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §4.8 K22):
- `range_tiles_depth` in `crates/px/src/range.rs` (SIMD128 `f64x2` + scalar), exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs`
  (ABI 31), wrapped in `renderer/editor/px/px.js` (`Px.rangeTilesDepth`, copying the `[wy0..wy1]` map and guide row
  window `[min(ry)..max(ry)] ± 2` bounded over the `[minTx..maxTx]` x span of the row's compute tiles — ~566 KB per
  upright tile row at 15,000 x 10,000, or the bounded `[minTx..maxTx]` y span on a turned map), JS twin in
  `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js` (with `libmExp(x)`, a direct IEEE 754 `f64` port of `libm-0.2.16/src/math/exp.rs` +
  `scalbn.rs` matching `px.wasm`'s `f64::exp` bit-for-bit in both Node and Electron 44 V8 15.2), routed in
  `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` under `"rangeDepth"` (`snap === false`) and `"rangeDepthSnap"` (`snap === true`) with
  split-routing support when one route is `"rust"` and the other `"js"`.
- `rangeSelectJob` (`source === "depth"`) in `renderer/editor/inpaint_worker.js` delegates `"compute"` tiles (`c === 2`)
  to `rangeTilesDepth` and merges the partial tiles' `alphaSum` and `bounds` with the `"full"` tiles' (`c === 1`)
  `rangeRowBookkeeping`.
- Route decisions by measurement (§1.1, 3 fresh-instance runs with `--rounds 3` per `tools/ab_median.py`):
  - `--ab rangeDepth=js:rust` (`after/ab_plain_r1..3.jsonl`): `verdict: rust` (`214.5 ms` vs `331.1 ms` wall, lower by
    `116.6 ms` > spread `107.1 ms`; worker job `1139.6 ms` vs `2013.5 ms`).
  - `--ab rangeDepthSnap=js:rust` (`after/ab_snap_r1..3.jsonl`): `verdict: rust` (`1174.4 ms` vs `1385.3 ms` wall, inside
    the `248.1 ms` wall spread because the window's pre-job picture `read` of `911.8-1136.3 ms` runs before any worker
    job is posted and is independent of the route; the worker-job `combined` mark drops from `396.7-452.8 ms` on `js` to
    `208.5-232.3 ms` on `rust`, and the kernel row drops from `3541.1 ms` to `1802.2 ms`).
  Both `rangeDepth` and `rangeDepthSnap` stay on Rust (`DEFAULT_ROUTES` carries no entry for either, documented above
  `DEFAULT_ROUTES` in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js`).
