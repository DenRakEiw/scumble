K23 measurement summary (2026-11-08)
====================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §4.8 K23):
- `range_tiles_rgba` in `crates/px/src/range.rs` (SIMD128 `f64x2` + scalar), exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs`
  (ABI 32), wrapped in `renderer/editor/px/px.js` (`Px.rangeTilesRgba`, copying only one tile row `[ry0..ry1)` =
  `(ry1 - ry0) * W * 4` bytes, at most 15.4 MB at 15,000 x 256, never the 61.4 MB 1024-row band), JS twin in
  `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js` (`rangeTilesRgba`, using three 256-entry `Float64Array` product tables for luma and
  `libmExp(-dd / twoS2)` for colour similarity so the twin and both wasm builds agree bit-for-bit in both Node and
  Electron 44 V8 15.2), routed in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` under `"rangeLuma"` (`mode === 0`) and `"rangeColor"`
  (`mode === 1`).
- Also added `i64x2_all_true(le_a)` and `i64x2_all_true(ge_b)` early exits to `sstep_f64x2` in `crates/px/src/range.rs`,
  skipping the `f64x2_div` and cubic polynomial whenever both lanes lie outside the feather transition `[a, b]` (speeding
  up both `range_tiles_rgba` and `range_tiles_depth` with identical bits).
- `rangeSelectJob` (`source === "luma" || source === "color"`) in `renderer/editor/inpaint_worker.js` loops over the band's
  tile rows `ty = startTileY .. endTileY` and delegates each tile row to `rangeTilesRgba(buf, W, H, ty, y0, y1, rowLimit, invert, TILE)`.
- Route decisions by measurement (§1.1, 3 runs with `--rounds 3` per `tools/ab_median.py`, run on each round's instance
  following `px_jobs`):
  - `--ab rangeLuma=js:rust` (`after/ab_luma_r1..3.jsonl`): `verdict: rust` (`1102.7 ms` vs `1893.4 ms` wall, lower by
    `790.7 ms` > spread `392.8 ms`; worker job `1033.6 ms` vs `2545.3 ms`).
  - `--ab rangeColor=js:rust` (`after/ab_color_r1..3.jsonl`): `verdict: rust` (`1976.6 ms` vs `3542.6 ms` wall, lower by
    `1566.1 ms` > spread `368.4 ms`; worker job `2893.4 ms` vs `6487.6 ms`).
  Both `rangeLuma` and `rangeColor` stay on Rust (`DEFAULT_ROUTES` carries no entry for either, documented above
  `DEFAULT_ROUTES` in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js`).
