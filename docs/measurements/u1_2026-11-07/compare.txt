Reading:
- Main no longer blocks on a model: its longest gap went from 1.2 s (the first SAM2 call), 0.2 s (each map's decoder
  batches), 0.3 s (Free VRAM), 2.6 s (a BiRefNet lite cutout on DirectML) and 0.7 s (a large depth map) to 0-25 ms.
  The 21-25 ms left are main relaying the answer to the window (a 4.5 MB label map, a 5.5 MB depth map, the 4 MB
  picture to the process: structured-clone copies, §3.2), under the sampler's 20 ms floor for the small answers.
- The renderer's IPC round trips during a job stay under 20 ms in the gpu batch (before: the block's length); in the
  editor batch the 68-72 ms are the window's own work (objectInput, the scale-back), as before.
- The waits did not get shorter (U1 moves the work, U2 shortens it). The gpu batch's are the same within a few percent.
  The editor batch's cached map took 1675 ms against 1432 (the job 1567 in the process against an automask of 1369 on
  main) and its point 161 against 51: one run each, not isolated. U0's probe saw the same for a map run first in a
  utility process (1.55-1.6 s, §1.4 of the plan), so the "B 64 first" advantage on main may not carry over; U2 item 4
  (the decoder's shapes) is where to measure it. Free VRAM's wall stays about 0.3 s (the process's exit with its
  DirectML allocations), but main is free throughout.
- Main's private bytes stay at 80-84 MB whatever runs: SAM2's 3.5-8.6 GB live in the helpers' process and go with it
  at Free VRAM, the idle unload or the quit.
