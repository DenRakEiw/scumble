The defect found and fixed (e4b8a77)
------------------------------------
C1's row showed no hint: Comfy-Org's Qwen Image 2.1 turbo LoRA (the plan's §6 Q4 file) carries 1-D `.diff` tensors of
attn.norm_q / attn.norm_k at the head's width 128, and electron/main/loraheader.js reduceHeader took them as the
transformer blocks' smallest out-features: width 128, so no family fit. A `.diff` of a 1-D tensor (a norm's scale) is
now left out of width and outs. Over all 1,204 LoRAs on the user's machine only that file's answer changed (none ->
"looks like Qwen Image 2.1"); after a restart of the dev instance its row read "looks like Qwen Image 2.1" in grey and
the 2509 row beside it stayed amber (G_loras_section_crop.png). tools/loraheader_test.js gained a fixture in the turbo
LoRA's naming (56 of 56).
