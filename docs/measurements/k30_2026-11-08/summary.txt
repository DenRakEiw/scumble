Stop 6 analysis and recommendation (whether K31 Rust liquify_dab is worth building):
  - K30's dense JS twin already reaches the plan's §4.9 target ("dab 20.6 -> 6-8 ms"): push fit size 1000 (which runs 4
    sub-dabs per pointermove) is down to 7.3 ms p50 (7.7 ms p95), and a single 1000 px dab at s = 4 (251 x 251 = 63,001
    nodes) is down to 1.7-1.8 ms p50 (push 1:1 size 1000: 1.8 ms; shrink held tick: 1.7 ms), including this.nodes(si0, sj0,
    sw, sh) gather and this._writeDense(...) cell scatter.
  - Because this.nodes(...) gather and _writeDense(...) cell scatter stay in JS either way, and a Rust liquify_dab would add
    wasm copy-in of src (~512 KB) and copy-out of out (~504 KB) per sub-dab, the remaining ceiling for K31 is at most ~0.4-0.5
    ms per 1000 px dab (~1.5-2.0 ms across the 4 sub-dabs at fit, where the whole frame + dab is already 8.2 ms, well under a
    16.6 ms 60 Hz frame), while K31 also carries the swirl sin/cos domain parity risk.
  - Recommendation for Stop 6: skip K31 and keep liquifyDab on the dense JS twin liquifyDabNodes ("stays JS, measured", as
    Stop 6 anticipated: "it may win back most of the Map cost"). The K.liquifyDab seam and ROUTE_KINDS.liquifyDab are in place
    if the user prefers to build K31 anyway.
