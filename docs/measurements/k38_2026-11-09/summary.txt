Byte effect
-----------
When `applyMatchGL` returns `null` (no WebGL2 context or a canvas side above `glContext().max`), `matchCanvas(src, stats, strength)` (`renderer/editor/inpaint_filters.js:272-298`) now routes its pixel loop through `matchPixels` (`renderer/editor/px/kernels.js`) in 4 MP (`MATCH_CHUNK_BYTES = 16,777,216` B = `4,194,304` px) chunks in place on `ImageData.data`. Each non-transparent channel is computed in `f32` (`floor(clamp(v + (m - v) * k, 0, 255) + 0.5)`), matching the WebGL2 shader and the worker's `matchPixels`, and differs by at most 1 byte level (`<= 1`) from the pre-K38 `f64` `Uint8ClampedArray` ties-to-even loop. When WebGL2 handles the canvas (`applyMatchGL` returns a canvas), bytes are unchanged.
