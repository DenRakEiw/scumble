Why the 2-lane `f64x2` block was removed (`K14-T1`):
- In the 4-arm 15,000 x 10,000 comparison (`summary_rows.txt`), `after_scalar` (`march_pixel_scalar` + `iy_rows` / `max_steps`
  `y` precompute) beat `after_lanes` (`f64x2` lanes + the same `y` precompute) outside spreads on `relight_one` (`493 [490-501]`
  vs `599 [570-608]` ms, blocked `42.5 [40.9-46.4]` vs `62 [62-63]` ms, kernel `3,648 [3,626-3,670]` vs `4,497 [4,323-4,548]` ms),
  `relight_one_job` (`3,245 [3,197-3,257]` vs `3,530 [3,511-3,733]` ms), `relight_one_rep` (`516 [507-531]` vs `576 [540-617]` ms),
  `relight_one_nofeed` (`468 [460-487]` vs `548 [547-571]` ms), `relight_sun` kernel (`865 [844-874]` vs `912 [904-1,027]` ms;
  landed `170 [166-172]` vs `182 [172-192]` ms touching at `172`), `relight_nodraw_job` (`3,146 [3,039-3,364]` vs `3,435
  [3,404-3,537]` ms), and `relight_draw_job` (`3,194 [3,024-3,354]` vs `3,546 [3,424-3,549]` ms), and had lower medians on
  `relight_three` (`1,009 [965-1,070]` vs `1,094 [1,052-1,101]` ms), `relight_nodraw` (`465 [463-564]` vs `535 [523-537]` ms),
  and `relight_draw` (`518 [485-565]` vs `526 [522-564]` ms).
- This confirmed what `391f03a3`'s `px_bench` showed (`scalar_f64` faster than `simd_f64` in 23 of 24 rows): K14's speed-up
  comes from hoisting the row-and-tap invariant `y` march (`iy_rows` / `max_steps`) out of the pixel loop, whereas pairing
  adjacent pixels in `f64x2` forces a terminated ray to keep stepping alongside an active neighbour lane and extracts 32-bit
  lane indices back to GPRs every step for scalar `u16` depth gathers. Per `docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md` §1.1, the ~145 lines of
  `unsafe` `f64x2` code were removed and `march_pixel_scalar` + the `y` precompute kept.
