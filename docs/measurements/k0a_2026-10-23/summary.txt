== Verdict for Stop 1 (inferred from one run each; K0c confirms)
- The PSD open blocks the window 14 s (readPsd's JS on the window): cluster D (K20) is worth more than the plan ranked.
- Every TIFF / PSD / ORA open spends 1.9-2.7 s storing the picture as a PNG (uploadPixels), where png_filter_rows
  (Rust) costs as much as deflate (photo 7,790 vs 5,203 summed): the writer's filter choice is a kernel candidate the
  plan does not list (K10 / K6 neighbourhood), and so is the store itself (§4.6).
- TIFF: JS LZW decode 6.7 s on a one-row-strip file (K16); convert of plain RGBA8 0.5-0.7 s where a copy would do and
  unpredict 0.6-1.1 s (K15); inflate 1.0-1.8 s of wait.
- Inflate: the browser's inflate runs about 555-560 MB/s (inflated) per worker and scales 2.9x on 8 workers: the bar a
  Rust inflate (K2) has to beat; the parted PNG read (K8) has its native route measured.
- Scumble's own PNG rows are Paeth (76 %) and Average (23 %): K6's SIMD unfilter goes wide over channels only.
- A real save costs 0.6-2.0 s beyond the stubbed rows, and breaks from about 0.7 GB (BUGS.md): the export save (G2,
  question 6.8) is now a fix, not an option.
