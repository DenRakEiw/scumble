== Verdict for Stop 1 (inferred from one run each; K0c confirms)
- The window's combine is most of select by range (76 % of depth's 1.6 s, 40 % of luma's 2.3 s) and 0.4 s of the wand's
  block: K5 (sel_combine) pays for range and the wand at once, as Stop 3 suspected.
- A fill layer with a depth limit costs about 11.5 s in a layered export at 15k (limitAlphaRows on the window; the PSD
  17.3 s): K25 is worth more than the plan ranked (after question 6.2).
- The bucket over a 15k band blocks the window 2.0 s in getImageData / putImageData (the canvas round trip): K37's
  tile route removes the readback, not only a kernel's time.
- Frequency separation blocks the window 2.4 s, 2.1 s of it the blur: K26 / K27 as planned.
- Relight's full march is about 4.2 s per light in the app, not 2.7-2.9: K12's stripes (and K13-K14) gain more.
- The cold first call of a fresh compile at 1.8-2.2x the warm one is a measured candidate for K1's losing Rust jobs.
- The retouch dabs at 1000 px cost 263-278 ms per move at fit (blur, sharpen): K26.
- Stitch at 4096^2: colorMatch, blur, dilate, warp and the encodes are the shares (K32-K34); the edge fit's shrink is
  larger than fitEdges itself.
