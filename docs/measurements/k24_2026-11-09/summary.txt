====================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §4.8 K24):
- `guided_far` and `guided_far_scratch_bytes` in `crates/px/src/guided.rs`, exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs` (ABI 37),
  built on `maskf::box_pass::<1>` with `Norm::Div((2 * r_work + 1) as f64)`:
  - Normalises and inverts `raw` (`rw * rh` `f32`) by `[lo, hi]` with the flat-range guard `hi - lo > 1e-12`.
  - When `(rw, rh) != (gw, gh)`, bilinearly resizes `far_f32` to `gw * gh` with pixel-centre coordinates `(x + 0.5) * ratio - 0.5`,
    clamped edges, `wx` stored as `f32` and promoted back to `f64` while `fy` stays `f64` (matching `inpaint_depth.js`
    `bilinearResize`).
  - Forms `I = ((grey[i] as f64) * (1.0 / 255.0)) as f32` and `Ip`, `II` from the raw `I` and `p` before any in-place box
    pass.
  - Runs 6 single-channel box passes (`mean_i`, `mean_p`, `mean_ip`, `mean_ii`, `mean_a`, `mean_b`), computes `av = cov_ip / (var_i + eps)`
    in `f64`, stores `a[i] = av as f32`, computes `b[i] = (mean_p - av * mean_i) as f32` from the unrounded `f64` `av`,
    clamps `mean_a * I + mean_b` to `0.0..=1.0` as `f32`, and quantises `Math.round(q[i] * 65535.0)` into `out` (`gw * gh` `u16`).
- Wrapped in `renderer/editor/px/px.js` (`Px.prototype.guidedFar`), JS twin in `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js` (`guidedFar`,
  delegating to `inpaint_depth.js` `guidedFar` with `boxBlurs`), routed in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` (`guidedFar`,
  route `"guidedFar"` in `ROUTE_KINDS`).
- `depthGuide` in `renderer/editor/inpaint_worker.js` and its two callers `computeDepthMap` and `depthFromLayer` in
  `renderer/editor/inpaint_canvas.js`:
  - The grey conversion loop (`Math.round(bytes[i] * 0.299 + bytes[i + 1] * 0.587 + bytes[i + 2] * 0.114)`) moved from the
    window thread (`computeDepthMap` and `depthFromLayer`) into `depthGuide` in the worker; `guide.image.buffer` (`gd`) is
    transferred to the worker and transferred back (`poolRes.gd`) so `makeMap` keeps the exact same `map.guide` `Uint8ClampedArray`
    bytes without cloning.
  - `disparityRange(raw, 0.005, 0.995)` moved from `computeDepthMap` into `depthGuide` when `lo` or `hi` is omitted, returning
    `{ lo, hi }` for `map.meta`; `depthFromLayer` continues to send `lo: 0, hi: 1`.
  - `inpaint_depth.js` `guidedFar`, `guidedFilter`, and `bilinearResize` are kept unchanged for reference and other callers;
    relight's `detailRatio` and haze's guided filter were not touched (no measured row shows them).
