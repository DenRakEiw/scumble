K17 measurement summary (2026-11-09)
====================================

Behaviour change (`rules.md` report checklist (a)):
- When opening a tiled TIFF with uncompressed, LZW, or PackBits compression whose tile height fits within 64 MiB of RGBA, a tile that decodes to fewer bytes than its rows is now refused with `tile N holds fewer bytes than its rows` rather than `tile N holds <got> of <want> bytes`.
- When opening a multi-strip or multi-tile TIFF whose band fits within 64 MiB of RGBA, every strip or tile range in the band is now checked against the file size before decoding the band, so a later strip or tile in that band that lies past the end of the file is reported (`strip/tile M lies past the end of the file`) ahead of an earlier strip or tile that is short or damaged inside the file.

Root-cause notes on ties and rows where `after` had higher numbers:
- `raw openMs` (`3,289.0 [3,257.0-3,323.0] -> 3,460.0 [3,416.0-3,533.0]` ms, `+171.0` ms): `adobe_15000x10000_raw.tif` is a single 10,000-row strip (`10,000 * 15,000 * 4 = 600,000,000` RGBA bytes > `TIFF_BAND_BYTES`), so `tiffPlan` sets `plan.streamed = true` and `readTiff` runs the unchanged `StripRows` fallback (`tiff_read kernel`: `482.6 [455.4-484.0] -> 494.4 [480.8-506.7]` ms, overlapping `+11.8` ms). Of the `+171.0` ms median difference in `openMs`, `+123.0` ms (`1,109.0 -> 1,232.0` ms) is in `openSteps stored` (`open png_part deflate`: `4,058.3 -> 4,559.4` ms, untouched tile-store PNG compression) and `+24.0` ms is in `openSteps base` (`440.0 -> 464.0` ms).
- `own` and `noise` `tiff_read wait` (`868.3 -> 935.8` ms and `1,361.7 -> 1,474.2` ms): in `readTiffBand`, `sp.wait` sums both the contiguous `blob.slice().arrayBuffer()` read and `DecompressionStream("deflate")` `reader.read()`. An initial prototype that piped `new Blob([rawSlice]).stream()` into `DecompressionStream` regressed `own` / `noise` / `w0_bench` by `+276..+627` ms due to worker-to-browser BlobRegistry Mojo IPC; replacing that with direct in-process `ds.writable.getWriter()` writes (`INFLATE_FEED_SLICE = 1 MiB`) and `ab.transfer(0)` eliminated the regression completely, making total `tiff_read kernel` and `openSteps pixels` faster than `before` in `3/3` pairs on both `own` (`1,348 -> 1,317` ms) and `noise` (`1,919 -> 1,880` ms).
