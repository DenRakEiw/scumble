K34 — Edgefit shrink, warp RGBA & warp coverage in Rust (2026-11-08)
====================================================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §4.10 & §5 K34)
----------------------------------------------
- Three edgefit kernels in `crates/px/src/maskf.rs` (scalar + wasm32 `simd128` in module `lanes`), exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs` (ABI bumped 26 -> 27):
  1. `edgefit_shrink(rgba_ptr, w, h, tw, th, g_ptr, a_ptr)`:
     - Fuses `grayOf`, `alphaOf`, and two `shrinkPlane` calls from `renderer/editor/edgefit.js` (`shrinkRGBA`).
     - When `tw == w && th == h` (1:1, used when `max(w, h) <= 512`), computes `alpha[i] = (rgba[4*i + 3] as f64 / 255.0) as f32` and `gray[i] = ((rgba[4*i + 3] as f64 / 255.0) * (0.299*R + 0.587*G + 0.114*B) / 255.0) as f32` (using the unrounded `f64` alpha in `gray[i]`, matching `edgefit.js:65-67`).
     - When `tw != w || th != h` (box-shrunk to `WORK = 512`), processes row band `[y0..y1)` per output row `ty`, computing temporary row `g_row` and `a_row` in `f32` (with unrounded `f64` alpha in `g_row`, and `a_row` rounded to `f32` first), then accumulates `sg` and `sa` in `f64` over `[y0..y1) x [x0..x1)` in identical order to `shrinkPlane` (`for sy in y0..y1 { for sx in x0..x1 }`), storing `g_out = (sg * inv) as f32`, `a_tmp = (sa * inv) as f32`, `a_out = if a_tmp > 1e-4 { a_tmp } else { 0.0 }`.
  2. `warp_rgba(src_ptr, dst_ptr, w, h, m_ptr)`:
     - Bilinear inverse-mapped RGBA8 warp matching `edgefit.js` `warpRGBA`:
       `xx = max(0, min(w - 1, a00*x + a01*y + tx))`, `yy = max(0, min(h - 1, a10*x + a11*y + ty))`
       (with `NaN` clamping to `0.0` via `if raw > 0.0 { raw.min(cap) } else { 0.0 }`),
       `x0 = min(w - 2, xx | 0)`, `y0 = min(h - 2, yy | 0)` (clamped to `0` when `w == 1` or `h == 1`),
       `fx = xx - x0`, `fy = yy - y0`,
       `top = p00*(1 - fx) + p10*fx`, `bot = p01*(1 - fx) + p11*fx`,
       `v = top*(1 - fy) + bot*fy`, stored with `Uint8ClampedArray` ties-to-even rounding.
  3. `warp_coverage(dst_ptr, w, h, m_ptr)`:
     - 4-edge coverage ramp matching `edgefit.js` `warpCoverage`:
       `xx = a00*x + a01*y + tx`, `yy = a10*x + a11*y + ty`,
       `d = min(xx + 1.0, yy + 1.0, (w - 1) - xx + 1.0, (h - 1) - yy + 1.0)`,
       `dst[y*w + x] = max(0.0, min(1.0, d)) as f32` (normalizing `-0.0` to `+0.0` and preserving V8's `0xffc0_0000` NaN bit pattern when `m` contains `NaN`).
- JS twins `edgefitShrink`, `warpRgba`, and `warpCoverage` in `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js`, `Px` wrappers in `renderer/editor/px/px.js`, and routed wrappers in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` under group route `"edgefit"` and per-kernel routes `"edgefitShrink"`, `"warpRgba"`, `"warpCoverage"`.
- Wired into `renderer/editor/edgefit.js` (`shrinkRGBA`, `warpRGBA`, `warpCoverage`, with `shrinkRGBA` also exported for direct testing).

4. Decision Rule (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §1.1) & K35 Split Analysis (§4.10)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
- **K34 routes**:
  - Whole-operation walls win across every benchmark: `edgefit_2048` `fit` `-70.93 ms` (`2.32x`) and `K34_sum` `-47.32 ms` (`1.43x`), `stitch_align_2048` worker wall `-142.00 ms` (`-18.9%`), `stitch_align_4096` worker wall `-367.06 ms` (`-14.9%`), cold `native_test provider_crop` wall `-41.00 ms` (`-7.6%`), and warmed `provider_crop` wall `-94.00 ms` (`-18.3%`, `finish.SUM` `-112.97 ms` / `-29.1%`).
  - All three kernels (`edgefit_shrink`, `warp_rgba`, `warp_coverage`) also win on `PX_BENCH` at `1492 x 1492` (`4.73x`, `1.18x`, `1.20x`). Note that on 2048^2 and 4096^2 in `w0_bench`, `warp` alone (`warpRGBA` + wasm copy-in/copy-out of 16.8 MB / 67.1 MB) ties the JS twin within ~1-5 ms on the worker (`81.42 ms` vs `77.63 ms` at 2048^2, `313.25 ms` vs `312.19 ms` at 4096^2; `78.05 ms` vs `79.37 ms` on window at 2048^2) and wins in `PX_BENCH` (`43.91 ms` vs `51.79 ms`, `1.18x`), so under §1.1 rule 1 & rule 2 all three stay on `"rust"` in `DEFAULT_ROUTES`.
- **K35 (`edgefit_levels`) conditional split analysis**:
  - Because `fitEdges` always operates on the shrunk `512 x 512` working plane (`WORK = 512`), its cost is `O(512^2)` and does not grow with document or crop size:
    - `native_test provider_crop` (1492^2 crop, early stop): `finish.fit` = **5.91 ms** warmed (**6.83 ms** cold), which is **2.2%** of `finish.SUM` (`274.59 ms`) and **1.4%** of `provider_crop` wall (`420.00 ms`).
    - `stitch_align_2048` (2048^2 crop, full 4-level `[8, 4, 2, 1]` x 8 Gauss-Newton steps): `worker.fit` = **41.45 ms** (**6.8%** of `609.98 ms` worker finish wall; **4.1%** of `1003.28 ms` prepare + finish wall); `window.fit` = **22.44 ms** (**3.7%** of `610.37 ms` window finish wall).
    - `stitch_align_4096` (4096^2 crop, full 4-level fit): `worker.fit` = **22.71 ms** (**1.1%** of `2103.73 ms` worker finish wall; **0.8%** of `2975.43 ms` prepare + finish wall); `window.fit` = **28.62 ms** (**1.3%** of `2156.67 ms` window finish wall).
    - `edgefit_2048` (standalone synthetic `fitLayerRGBA` without stitch mask/blur/colorMatch/encode): `split.fitEdges` = **31.09 ms** out of `53.66 ms` `fitLayerRGBA` (**57.9%** of `fitLayerRGBA` alone now that `shrink` is `21.58 ms`, and **21.3%** of the `146.18 ms` `shrink + ring + fitEdges + warp + coverage` sum).
  - **Recommendation**: In real stitch operations (`provider_crop`, `stitch_align_2048`, `stitch_align_4096`), `fitEdges` is **6–41 ms** (**1.1%–6.8%** of `finishResultAsync` wall, **0.8%–4.1%** of prepare + finish wall), smaller than the run-to-run spread of `stitch_align`. Recommend **skipping K35 ("stays JS, measured")** unless you want the remaining ~15–25 ms on standalone `fitLayerRGBA` / `Match edges` (`match_edges`) captured.
