What each run did
-----------------
- void runs (my error, not the recipe's): the driver called set_prompt with `prompt` instead of `text`, so both went
  with an empty prompt (`prompt_sent: ""`, the canvas state's prompt ""). Without the upsampler the node's "Edit
  <Picture 1>." alone gave a new picture of the same things (a blue car before a colonnade, 75 levels mean difference
  over the crop; run_void_empty_prompt_crop_vs_answer.png). With the upsampler, the request "Edit <Picture 1>. " with
  no text made the upsampler invent an edit ("<Subject 1> is replaced with a red sports car"). Nothing in Scumble
  refuses an empty prompt on this recipe (an open point, PLAN_PH3.md section 4).
- a (upsampler off): NOT an edit. The model drew a new picture: a sunset over a city with trees, nothing of the
  building kept (63 levels mean difference over the crop, 64 in its lower 40 %; run_a_crop_vs_answer.png). The queued
  conditioning got the prompt as typed (`cond.prompt = ["canvas", 7]`, the up_* nodes and the switch gone), so the
  node put "Edit <Picture 1>. " in front. This is what Pruna AI's card says of short requests without the upsampler;
  here it happened on the first try.
- b (upsampler on): the edit asked for. The sky turned into an orange sunset, the building, the tree and the car stayed
  (1.4 levels mean difference in the crop's lower 40 %, 22.6 over the whole crop with the sky). The request was
  Pruna AI's chat block "<Picture 1>: <|vision_start|><|image_pad|><|vision_end|>Edit <Picture 1>. make the sky a warm
  sunset"; the upsampler wrote subject_definitions / summary ("the sky is replaced with a warm sunset") /
  retention_analysis ("building and car preserved") / detailed_description; the status line showed its start and the
  log its whole text (queued_graphs.json, outputs.up_show). The node's own align: "no gain" (1.44 before and after),
  the stitch fits the 4 MP answer back with no shift.
- c (one reference): `prompt_sent` "put the blue bag from <Picture 2> on the window sill"; the conditioning got
  `images.image_1` (the crop through scale0) and `images.image_2` (the reference), every other slot left out; the
  upsampler's request had two picture blocks, <Picture 1> and <Picture 2>, and it named Picture 2 "a blue woven leather
  handbag with a gold chain". The answer kept the facade (4.5 levels mean difference) and put that bag on the sill
  left of the cat. The app's edge fit aligned it by (-0.8, -0.7) px, scale 1.0036 / 1.003. The model has no mask
  input and placed the bag across the selection's right edge, so the feathered edge cut its right side
  (run_c_stitch_zoom.png): a selection with room around the place is the user's part.
- d (Generate new, no reference): a real text-to-image run. The conditioning had no `images.image_1`, the upsampler no
  picture input, its request "Aspect ratio: 16:9\nRequest: a lighthouse on a rocky coast at dawn, photograph"; it wrote
  a long photographic caption; the picture is a lighthouse at sunrise, 1024 x 576, no white anywhere
  (run_d_generate_new.png).
- e (8 MP, a prompt that already starts "Edit <Picture 1>"): the request went without a second "Edit <Picture 1>. "
  (`verbatim` works); the bag sits on the sill left of the cat, inside the selection, the rest kept (6.0 levels mean
  difference, the box outlines drawn on the picture kept too). 52 s, no out-of-memory.
- f (Generate new with a reference layer, review finding 4): the white canvas went as <Picture 1> and the bag as
  <Picture 2>; the upsampler called Picture 1 "empty white canvas", "weak_reference - blank canvas", and the picture is
  the bag (woven pattern and gold chain as in the reference) on a wooden garden table in sunlight, no white left
  (run_f_generate_new_with_reference.jpg). The blank first picture composes well: no renumbering is needed. It costs
  time: 47 s against 20 s without it (two pictures at 4 MP in the conditioning).
