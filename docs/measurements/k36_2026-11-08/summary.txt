K36 measurement summary (2026-11-08)
====================================

Scope (docs/PLAN_WASM_BUILD.md §4.5 K36):
- `composite_masked_tile` in `crates/px/src/composite.rs` (`masked_tile`, exported from `crates/px/src/lib.rs`, ABI 32,
  wrapped in `renderer/editor/px/px.js` `Px.compositeMaskedTile`, JS twin in `renderer/editor/px/kernels_js.js`
  `compositeMaskedTile`, routed in `renderer/editor/px/kernels.js` `compositeMaskedTile` by route `compositeMaskedTile`):
  - Composites `src` (`px * 4` straight RGBA8 bytes) over `dst` (`px * 4` straight RGBA8 bytes, or `has_dst == 0` when
    the destination tile does not exist yet and is taken as 0) under `op` (`0..=30`) and `alpha` (`0..255`) with
    optional coverage `mask` (`px` bytes), keeping `dst` (or 0) untouched where `src` has alpha 0 or `mask` is 0.
  - Processes in 256-pixel (`1024`-byte) chunks, finding each chunk's active 4-pixel-aligned span `(x0, x1)` via
    `simd::active_span`, skipping inactive chunks/prefixes/suffixes without premultiplying/unpremultiplying untouched
    pixels, running `begin` + `apply` + `end` on `x0..x1`, and restoring inactive pixels inside `x0..x1` from `orig`
    while accumulating `changed` and `non_empty` via `simd::finish_masked` + scalar tail.
  - Returns `UNCHANGED` (`0`) when no pixel changed, `WRITTEN` (`1`) when changed and at least one pixel has alpha > 0
    afterwards (calling `write(true)` once to obtain the destination buffer and copy out the whole `px * 4` bytes),
    `EMPTY` (`2`) when changed and every pixel has alpha 0 afterwards, or `REFUSED` (`3`) when arguments are out of
    range.
- `TileLayerPixels.compositeStroke` in `renderer/editor/inpaint_tiles.js` calls `compositeMaskedTile(old ? old.data :
  null, (whole) => this.writable(tx, ty, true, whole && TILE_SIZE === 256).data, s.data, op, alpha, cov, TILE_SIZE *
  TILE_SIZE)` instead of `compositeTile` on `STROKE_SCRATCH` + the 65,536-iteration JS loop:
  - Unchanged tiles (`r === 0`) are never made writable (kept shared with any frozen undo clone).
  - Emptied tiles (`r === 2`) are dropped (`_dropTile(key)`) without allocating or copying a writable tile.
  - Written tiles (`r === 1`) call `writable(tx, ty, true, true)`, skipping the prior 256 KB copy of a frozen tile
    before overwriting all `256² * 4` bytes.
