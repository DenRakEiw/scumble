Scumble

A free, open-source desktop editor built around AI models. Inpaint, edit, upscale and generate: select, say what should be there, and the result comes back as a layer of its own, colour-matched to its surroundings.

One picture from the first generation to the finished frame, 17 layers, without leaving the editor (sped up, the waits cut out).

What it does

  • Select by rectangle, brush, lasso, magic wand, object hover, a text description, or by depth, brightness and colour; type a prompt; the result lands as a new layer, colour-matched, with masks, 27 blend modes and an undo step of its own. Several separate areas can run one after another, each at its own size.
  • Draw boxes on an empty canvas or on the picture, a caption each, and the picture is generated to that layout (FLUX 3 Image, its open Klein model and MOSS-Image on your own ComfyUI, Ideogram 4). Reference layers are named in the prompt as @img1, @img2.
  • Layers, groups, masks, clipping; filter layers (curves, colour grading, HSL, grain, 46 film looks, lens blur, haze and dehaze on a depth map) with a live scope and a clipping warning; text layers; retouch tools (heal, remove, liquify, dodge and burn, frequency separation); in-app cutout, depth maps and automatic layer names.
  • Relight with virtual lights from the picture's depth map. Deep paint: paint, a light brush and wrapped logos that take the picture's own light and keep its reflections. Contact shadows under cut-outs; 3D objects and a posable mannequin as depth and pose guides.
  • Upscale the selection or the whole picture; a Realism Pass for generated skin, hair and fabric; green and blue screen keying with CorridorKey 2.0; one picture split into layers; outpainting in one step; a frame from a video as a picture or a layer.
  • Pictures up to 15,000 pixels stay fluid; export to PNG, JPEG, WebP, TIFF, and layered PSD and ORA.
  • The pixels come from your own ComfyUI (local or remote, with your LoRAs), Comfy Cloud, or image APIs on your own keys (FLUX 3 Image, FLUX.2, GPT Image, Nano Banana, Seedream, Qwen, Ideogram, Krea and more). No account, no subscription; keys stay in the OS credential store.
  • Every command is an MCP tool, 124 of them: Claude Desktop installs it with one click from the release's .mcpb, Claude Code and any MCP client drive the window you are looking at, and --run works through a folder of files as a batch. A built-in assistant on your own key uses the same commands.
